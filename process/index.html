How I work

The same five steps, at every company and for every customer.

  1. 1

    Start with people

    Talk to users and customers, and see how they work and what they need to do their job.

  2. 2

    Make sense of it

    Analyze what I heard and align on a direction the business agrees with.

  3. 3

    Go low fidelity fast

    Wireframe, test with users, and get feedback early.

  4. 4

    Design with engineering

    Work together early and often, so what I design can be built.

  5. 5

    Ship, listen, iterate

    Then iterate again.

Then reality arrives

That's the process. It rarely survives a real deadline intact. A customer threatens to leave, a demo date appears, a team gets reassigned.

So I don't retell these five steps in every case study. Each one shows where the process bent on that project, what I did instead, and why the outcome was still right for the people using it.
